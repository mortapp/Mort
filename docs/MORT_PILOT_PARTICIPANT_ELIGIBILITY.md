# MORT Pilot Participant Eligibility

Status: implementation-aligned eligibility draft. The hosted function `get_closed_pilot_eligibility` is the technical authority; authorized operators remain responsible for lawful program decisions.

## All participants

Participants need an active Supabase Auth account, completed age-appropriate profile, current closed-pilot enrollment, approved organization route, confirmed email, and current policy acknowledgements. Enrollment, policy version, expiration, environment, and account restrictions are checked server-side.

## Teens

Teens must be 13-17, confirm contact access, complete teen safety training, accept pilot rules, and provide explicit consent. A guardian connection, permanent address, government ID, conventional school ID, bank account, vehicle, or Support Circle is not a universal requirement.

## Adults and businesses

Adults must be 18 or older and are not admitted as unrestricted public posters. They need an approved organization relationship, confirmed email and phone, adult safety training, prohibited-work, payment/scope, incident, and pilot-rule acknowledgements, plus manual pilot approval. Business claims require separate registry and representative review where applicable.

## Guardians and support contacts

Guardian Mode is optional. A Support Circle is separate, teen-controlled, and does not affect profile completion or independent marketplace eligibility. Program-specific lawful supervision must be explicit, narrowly configured, and reviewed rather than silently applied to every teen.

## Revocation and expiration

Expired enrollment, revoked invitation, withdrawn partner authority, account restriction, incomplete current acknowledgements, or a failed job-specific decision can deny a protected action. Revoking one attestation removes only the associated trust signal unless a separate, documented safety decision supports broader restriction.

# MORT Future Independence Policy

Status: product and safety draft. It is not financial, housing, legal, educational, or crisis advice.

## Purpose

Future Independence Plan supports lawful preparation for adulthood: savings, education, employment, resume and references, transportation, identification checklists, budgeting education, housing-cost concepts, renter terminology, utilities, emergency funds, trusted support, workforce referrals, and age-18 account transition.

## Prohibited guidance

MORT must not provide instructions for a minor to secretly leave home, evade lawful protections, hide from guardians or authorities, obtain false documents, conceal location from emergency responders, or enter unsafe housing or work. The backend stores `runaway_guidance_provided = false` as an invariant.

## Privacy

Plans, tasks, and goals are owner-only and private by default. They do not appear on a public profile, poster view, ad segment, partner roster, Support Circle alert, or job ranking. Do not infer abuse, homelessness, or family conflict from plan contents.

## Immediate danger

Show local emergency guidance, qualified crisis/support resources, Safety Ping, and optional trusted-contact notification. State clearly that MORT is not monitored continuously and does not replace emergency services, shelters, social workers, legal counsel, or healthcare.

## Financial truth

Earnings can be self-recorded and goals can be planned, but MORT does not hold, escrow, guarantee, process, or take custody of job payments. Do not present a self-recorded amount as bank-verified income.

## Age-18 transition

Account migration requires a future reviewed procedure for permissions, consent, retained youth records, guardian/support links, age-based job limits, and identity signals. Do not broaden access automatically at midnight on a birthday without tested policy.

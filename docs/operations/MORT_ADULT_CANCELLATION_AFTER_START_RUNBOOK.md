# Adult Cancellation After Start Runbook

1. Confirm the adult is the job poster and the accepted job has a verified start.
2. Require a factual reason; do not promise a refund or compensation.
3. Preserve the contract, payment obligation, execution events, proof, messages authorized for the case, and participant statements.
4. The server records cancellation and opens human review. It calculates only a policy recommendation.
5. Do not move money automatically.
6. Check for safety reports, scope change, adult refusal, provider dispute, and participant response.
7. A reviewer recommends; a separate operator executes only through server-owned idempotent provider functions when enabled.
8. Record notice, appeal route, and final provider reconciliation.

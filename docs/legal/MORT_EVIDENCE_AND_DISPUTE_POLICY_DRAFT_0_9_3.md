# MORT Evidence and Dispute Policy Draft 0.9.3

## Permitted evidence

Users may provide factual statements, before and after photos, work-result photos, screenshots of relevant in-app MORT messages, time notes, and references to start or finish events. Images are private, re-encoded, metadata-stripped, size-limited, hashed, and served through short-lived authorized URLs. Users must not submit unrelated identity documents, card data, exact addresses, archives, executables, or unrelated private messages.

Evidence submission does not guarantee payment or any outcome. MORT may review relevant MORT records and authorized evidence under the accepted policy. AI does not determine truth, fraud, guilt, punishment, refunds, or transfers.

## Disputes and preservation

Adult funding occurs before work; MORT does not guarantee work quality. Payment release may be delayed during a dispute. PINs record actions but do not guarantee identity or quality. Adult after-start cancellation and non-safety teen abandonment can lead to partial or no payment after review; safety cancellation cannot create an automatic penalty.

Submitted dispute evidence can be placed on preservation hold and may no longer be immediately removable. Reviewer access is audited. Ordinary support retention differs from active dispute evidence retention.

MORT decisions are platform-contract decisions, not court judgments or criminal findings. Appeals may be available. Stripe eligibility and provider guardian requirements remain applicable. Support and AI are not emergency, medical, or legal services. Email is a manual fallback and must not include secrets or sensitive identity documents. Terms change only by versioning and reacceptance. This draft is inactive.

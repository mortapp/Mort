# MORT Emergency Disclosure Process Draft

Status: draft requiring counsel, trained 24/7 ownership, recipient validation, and tabletop testing. MORT is not an emergency service.

## Threshold

Only designated legal/safety staff may consider a voluntary emergency disclosure. Counsel must define the applicable legal standard and MORT's provider status. In the United States, 18 U.S.C. 2702 includes provider exceptions tied to a good-faith belief that an emergency involving danger of death or serious physical injury requires disclosure without delay; applying that text to a real request requires case-specific legal review.

## Intake Questions

- Who is at risk, what is the danger, and why is it imminent?
- What facts support the request beyond a conclusory emergency label?
- What data is requested, how will it address the danger, and why can ordinary process not arrive in time?
- Is the requester a validated governmental entity and reachable through an independently verified channel?
- Is there an existing MORT incident, active job, location share, arrival mismatch, threat, abduction concern, or Safety Ping?

## Decision

1. Page the incident manager and legal-request reviewer.
2. Validate requester identity and document the emergency facts.
3. Determine whether disclosure is legally permitted and necessary; do not treat a request as automatically valid.
4. Limit content, account, and location data to the minimum responsive scope.
5. Require two-person approval when time and safety allow; document why a single authorized responder acted if not.
6. Transfer through an approved secure channel and confirm receipt.

## After Action

Create a restricted timeline event, preserve relevant records, retain the request and approval basis, review whether user notice is permitted/required, and conduct a prompt post-incident audit. Correct any overproduction, access failure, or recipient error under the breach/incident process.

## User-Facing Boundary

The app directs users to call local emergency services for immediate danger. A Safety Ping or report does not guarantee that MORT staff, guardians, contacts, or law enforcement will see or act on it in time.

## Primary Legal Starting Point

- Current U.S. Code section 2702: https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/2702

This draft must not be used as a substitute for legal advice or an emergency-response contract.

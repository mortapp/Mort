# MORT Legal Information, Not Advice Policy

> **DRAFT — NOT ATTORNEY REVIEWED OR LEGALLY APPROVED**

MORT may provide general, official, jurisdiction-tagged links and explain its own records and processes. It does not diagnose a legal claim, decide whether someone is an employee or contractor, predict success, select a court, calculate a filing deadline, draft individualized pleadings, threaten arrest or litigation, represent a user, or promise recovery.

Classification values are limited to classification_unknown, possible_employee_relationship, possible_independent_service_relationship, organization_program_role, and requires_review. They are routing warnings, not legal conclusions.

Employee wage-claim information appears only as potentially relevant general information. Small-claims, mediation, legal-aid, bar-referral, child-labor, and emergency resources must link to an allowlisted official source and include jurisdiction and last-review metadata. A user is told to seek licensed counsel for individualized advice.

Automated content must never produce a legal conclusion from age, role, job title, payment method, or dispute status. High-risk questions route to trained support with a clear scope boundary.

# MORT Product Master Plan

## North Star

Help a teen complete appropriate local work safely, learn from it, and build a trusted record while giving an adult or business a reliable way to define, staff, coordinate, and close that work.

The primary outcome is not time in app. It is a safe, mutually acknowledged work outcome with clear expectations, participant-only communication, accountable evidence when required, and a truthful history.

## Product Principles

1. Safety, privacy, accessibility, report, block, Safety Ping, basic Guardian Mode, browsing, applying, valid-participant messaging, and proof submission stay free.
2. Guardian Mode is optional by default and bounded by teen-visible sharing choices.
3. Server authority protects cross-user and high-impact actions.
4. Trust indicators show provenance and limits; they do not imply guaranteed safety.
5. AI assists with explanation and drafting but never infers sensitive traits or makes final high-impact decisions.
6. Revenue comes from optional convenience and business value, not blocked participation or pressure.
7. Every release claim is limited to evidence actually obtained.

## Execution Order

- **Foundation:** finish incomplete safety-critical contracts, parity, recovery, accessibility, and operations.
- **Repeat value:** work history, references, reusable jobs, trusted workers, schedules, goals, and healthy reminders.
- **Differentiation:** Skill Passport, Local Talent Bench, optional Guardian boundaries, explainable Opportunity Radar, and community partners.
- **Scale:** admin case ownership, appeals, policy versions, privacy-preserving analytics, balanced neighborhood launch tools.
- **Native release:** Xcode compile, iPhone verification, APNs, purchases, ads/consent, TestFlight, App Store disclosures.

## Outcome Metrics And Guardrails

| Outcome | Guardrail |
| --- | --- |
| Appropriate jobs viewed and applied to | Exclude prohibited work, blocked users, test data, and unsafe location exposure |
| Application response and fill rate | Track fairness and silent rejection; do not rank teens by sensitive traits |
| Completion and repeat relationship rate | Monitor cancellation, dispute, report, and incident rates together |
| Review/reference/skill history growth | Require provenance and correction routes; prevent retaliation and manipulation |
| Guardian feature usefulness | Track voluntary use and unlinking; never score teens by guardian participation |
| Support and safety resolution | Track time, quality, appeal, and recurrence; raw speed cannot override due process |
| Revenue | Measure free-path health, dismissal, refunds, failed purchase recovery, and safety parity |

## Current Program Status

The remaining 1,875 accepted records are roadmap capabilities, not an implementation claim. Release expansion remains gated by legal review, operations staffing, Mac compilation, physical-device testing, and TestFlight.

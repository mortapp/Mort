# Competitive Product Notes

MORT borrows product patterns, not branding or UI.

## Taskrabbit

Borrow:

- job/task lifecycle
- adult posting flow
- applicant review
- proof/completion state

Refuse:

- adult-style unrestricted gig categories for teens
- off-platform payment pressure

## Nextdoor

Borrow:

- local trust
- neighborhood-style discovery
- lightweight profiles

Refuse:

- open-ended community posting that becomes hard to moderate for teens

## Rover / Wag

Borrow:

- proof photos
- care notes
- trust badges
- check-in pattern

Refuse:

- any care category not legally/safely reviewed for teen workers

## Babysitting / Care Apps

Borrow:

- guardian trust
- emergency contacts
- parent oversight

Refuse:

- private off-platform coordination before safety tooling is stronger

## Fiverr / Upwork

Borrow:

- portfolio
- badges
- skills
- saved jobs
- profile strength

Refuse:

- pay-to-win visibility that makes teen earning dependent on purchases

## Teen Job Apps

Borrow:

- availability
- application tracker
- job readiness guidance

Refuse:

- unsafe job categories or unsupported labor-law assumptions

## Subscription Apps

Borrow:

- clear restore purchases
- manage subscription
- premium badges
- honest setup-empty states

Refuse:

- dark patterns, fake scarcity, manipulative countdowns

## Ad-Supported Apps

Borrow:

- low-risk banner placements
- frequency caps
- test ads during development

Refuse:

- ads on safety, chat, proof, guardian approval, payment, admin, or report screens

## What Makes MORT Unique

MORT combines a local teen-safe job marketplace, Guardian Mode, safe messaging scanner, proof of work, reports/blocking, admin moderation, payment preference-only flow, RevenueCat premium features, and AdMob with teen-safety controls.

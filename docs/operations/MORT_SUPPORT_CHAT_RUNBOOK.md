# Support Chat Runbook

1. Confirm the staff member has a current server-issued support role and case assignment.
2. Use the support queue; never request passwords, payment credentials, government IDs, or exact addresses in chat.
3. Treat AI text as labeled assistance only. Verify facts in authorized case views.
4. Route immediate danger to the safety-critical flow and emergency guidance; pause ordinary troubleshooting.
5. Request evidence only through the private upload flow.
6. Record status changes, assignments, and resolution reasons in the case.
7. Use the email fallback only as a draft with case number and data warning; email does not prove receipt/import.
8. Escalate privacy, child-safety, payment, or legal questions to the designated owner.
9. Close only after a human confirms the response and retention requirements.

External AI and inbound email sync are disabled in the current environment.

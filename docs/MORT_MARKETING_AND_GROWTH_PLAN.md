# MORT Marketing And Growth Plan

## Launch Unit

Launch by a bounded neighborhood or partner network only when both safe opportunity supply and teen interest exist. A waitlist is a capacity signal, not artificial scarcity.

## Acquisition Paths

- Schools, counselors, clubs, youth programs, libraries, nonprofits, and community centers
- Local businesses with seasonal, event, tutoring, technology-help, pet-help, and light nonhazardous needs after policy review
- Adult, teen, guardian, and partner referrals with fraud controls and no guaranteed reward claim
- Public-safe job and organization pages with QR links that expose no exact teen location or private account data
- Community event pages and recurring local opportunity calendars
- Educational content about job briefs, first-job habits, scams, prohibited work, and respectful communication

## Balanced Growth Metrics

Measure activated teens and posters, appropriate-job coverage, response time, fill/completion/repeat rates, geographic balance at a coarse level, support load, reports, blocks, incidents, cancellations, notification opt-outs, and partner retention. Stop expansion when safety/operations capacity, appropriate-job supply, or legal coverage is inadequate.

## Claims Policy

Never claim guaranteed earnings, guaranteed staffing, guaranteed safety, background screening that did not occur, legal compliance by default, or community endorsement without authorization. Testimonials need consent and may not expose a minor's private identity, location, messages, or evidence.

# MORT Support Privacy and Retention Draft 0.9.3

## Private support

A requester sees only their own support cases. Sharing a job does not reveal another participant's support conversation. Optional Guardian Mode does not provide universal support access. Assigned support and safety staff receive least-privilege, expiring access; message, evidence, status, and signed-URL access is audited.

Support users should provide only information needed for the case. Do not submit passwords, verification codes, full card numbers, payment tokens, exact residential addresses, government IDs, or unrelated private messages. Email to MORT is a manual fallback and is not automatically received, linked, or secured as in-app evidence.

## Retention and product boundaries

Ordinary support data uses a limited retention schedule. Evidence linked to an active dispute may be preserved longer and placed on hold. A submitted item may no longer be immediately removable. Evidence does not guarantee an outcome, and AI does not decide truth.

Adults fund accepted Stripe jobs before work. MORT does not guarantee work quality. Payment release may be delayed during disputes. PINs record actions, not identity. Adult after-start cancellation and non-safety teen abandonment may lead to partial or no payment after review; safety cancellation must not produce an automatic penalty.

MORT may review relevant authorized records under the accepted policy. Platform decisions are not court judgments or criminal findings; appeals may be available. Stripe and provider guardian rules still apply. Support and AI are not emergency, legal, or medical services. Terms change through versioning and required reacceptance. This draft is inactive.

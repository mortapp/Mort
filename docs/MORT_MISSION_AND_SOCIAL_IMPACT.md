# MORT Mission and Social Impact

Status: implementation-aligned policy draft. This document is not legal, privacy, labor, child-safety, or program approval.

## Mission

MORT helps teenagers gain legitimate income, work experience, practical skills, references, and safe pathways toward adulthood.

The product is designed to reduce barriers for teens who have limited income, transportation, work history, stable housing, traditional school records, or trusted support. Safety controls must remain available regardless of purchasing status, Guardian Mode, or Support Circle participation.

## Product commitments

- Teens ages 13-17 may pursue only age-appropriate, reviewed pilot work.
- Guardian Mode and Support Circle are optional under ordinary MORT rules.
- No permanent residential address, bank account, vehicle, government ID, or traditional school ID is a universal onboarding requirement for teens.
- Housing, abuse, foster, shelter, counselor, school, and family circumstances are never public profile or ranking signals.
- Report, block, Safety Ping, cancellation for safety, and emergency guidance are never paywalled.
- MORT does not hold, escrow, process, guarantee, or take custody of job payments.
- MORT does not provide instructions for a minor to run away, conceal unlawful conduct, or evade lawful protection.

## Economic mobility outcomes

The pilot should measure access without turning vulnerability into analytics inventory. Permitted aggregate measures include completed eligible jobs, references requested, skills recorded, voluntary savings-goal use, and partner-supported enrollments. Reports must use minimum cohort sizes and suppress small cells that could expose a participant.

MORT must not optimize job ranking, ads, prices, or partner visibility using housing status, resource use, crisis activity, Safety Ping use, document-review evidence, or inferred vulnerability.

## Launch boundary

The public marketplace is closed. Initial real-world use requires qualified partner organizations, trained and accountable adult operators, reviewed employers, a staffed incident process, and completion of legal, privacy, youth-labor, accessibility, and child-safety review. Passing technical QA alone does not open the marketplace.

## Review cadence

Mission impact and unintended harms should be reviewed before each pilot cohort and at least monthly during a live pilot. A qualified youth-safety lead must be able to pause a category, partner, organization, or the whole pilot without a growth or revenue override.

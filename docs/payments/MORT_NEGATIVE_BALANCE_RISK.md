# Negative Balance Risk

With separate charges and transfers, MORT's platform can bear refunds, disputes, chargebacks, Stripe fees, transfer reversals, payout failures, and unrecoverable connected-account balances. A successful transfer or payout does not eliminate later dispute exposure.

Before live funding, the owner must define reserves, maximum job amount, delayed-transfer policy, refund authority, dispute-response staffing, recovery limits, accounting treatment, and a platform funding source. Minors must not be threatened with debt collection or have safety access removed because of a provider balance. Automatic retries and reversals require caps and human review.

Current control: all live payment, transfer, refund, and payout gates are false. This risk is operationally unresolved and blocks live activation.

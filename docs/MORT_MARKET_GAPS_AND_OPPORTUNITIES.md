# MORT Market Gaps and Opportunities

## Highest-Value Gaps

1. **Teen-specific local work trust:** General neighborhood and freelance products do not combine age-aware job policy, school-time boundaries, private evidence, verified-job reviews, and optional guardian support.
2. **Small-job adult utility:** Adults and local businesses need concise job briefs, applicant decisions, reusable requirements, scheduling, proof, repeat-worker lists, and closeout without enterprise hiring overhead.
3. **Portable early work history:** Teens need a truthful bridge from local work to references, skills, resumes, and later employment without making sensitive evidence public.
4. **Healthy marketplace retention:** Most engagement systems reward attention. MORT can reward safe completion, learning, responsiveness, and repeat relationships while respecting pauses.
5. **Community partner infrastructure:** Schools, counselors, nonprofits, event organizers, and local businesses can create trusted opportunity supply if roles, public pages, reporting, and operational ownership are defined.
6. **Explainable safety automation:** Rule and AI assistance can flag risky descriptions or messages, but MORT can differentiate by showing reason, uncertainty, appeal, and human authority.
7. **Accessible work participation:** Accessibility needs to cover discovery through proof and support, not just onboarding or color contrast.

## Near-Term Opportunity

Finish trusted core work before broad novelty: portfolio contracts, adult operational summaries, APNs delivery, admin evidence timelines, upload recovery, and physical-device accessibility. These increase real marketplace completion and reduce support risk.

## Conditions Before Expansion

- Counsel-reviewed jurisdiction rules and prohibited-work taxonomy
- Moderation/support staffing and escalation coverage
- Mac/Xcode and physical-iPhone verification
- TestFlight feedback and incident telemetry
- Privacy and retention review for portfolio, verification, proof, analytics, marketing, and AI
- Balanced local launch supply; no guaranteed-income or guaranteed-safety claims

# MORT Reviewer Training Requirements

Status: training specification draft. Completion records and qualified instructors do not yet exist.

## Required training

Before access, reviewers need role-specific training in data minimization, precise verification claims, evidence limitations, youth privacy, trauma-informed communication, bias and accessibility, conflict checks, account sharing indicators, neutral fraud language, secure workspace use, vault access, audit obligations, retention, deletion, preservation locks, appeals, breach response, and applicable mandated-reporting procedure.

## Practical assessment

Reviewers must pass scenario exercises that distinguish affiliation from identity, age evidence from full identity, registry matching from representative identity, and suspicious evidence from a criminal accusation. They must demonstrate denial of unnecessary access, reason writing, redaction discipline, and safe escalation.

## Access lifecycle

Training approval must identify instructor, version, scope, date, expiration, and approver. Access is time-bounded and revoked for role change, stale training, policy breach, unresolved conflict, or no operational need. Ordinary admins and founder/developer status do not grant raw evidence access.

## Quality controls

Sample decisions should receive regular blind quality review. Track reversals, inconsistent labels, unnecessary data requests, access denials, appeal outcomes, and disparate impacts without exporting raw evidence. High-impact cases require two people and no self-approval.

## Minimum staffing

A live operation needs enough trained reviewers for separation of duties, appeal review, child-safety escalation, incident coverage, and leave. One founder or developer cannot serve as unchecked uploader, reviewer, approver, security operator, and appeal authority.

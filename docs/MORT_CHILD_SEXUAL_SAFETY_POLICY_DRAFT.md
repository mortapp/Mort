# MORT Child Sexual Safety Policy Draft

Status: draft requiring child-safety counsel, trained moderation, reporting-procedure approval, and App Store review. It is not a claim that MORT can prevent all abuse.

## Prohibited Conduct

MORT prohibits sexual solicitation of a minor, grooming, sexualized job requests, requests for nudity or sexual images, sexual services, coercion, sextortion, child sexual abuse material, trafficking, in-person sexual contact, secrecy pressure, migration to unsafe off-platform channels, sexualized age-role play, and retaliation for reporting.

The prohibition applies to adults, teens, businesses, guardians, profiles, jobs, applications, messages, attachments, reviews, payment notes, and off-platform conduct connected to MORT use.

## Product Controls

- Identity and age verification are required for marketplace participation.
- Messaging is limited to authorized job participants and passes through a server safety scanner.
- Sexual adult-minor language, threats, grooming indicators, and clearly prohibited job content are blocked or escalated.
- Report and block remain free; blocking closes ordinary contact.
- Exact locations are staged and Safety Agreements, person-match arrival checks, check-ins, and safety cancellation support real-world exit.
- Serious reports preserve relevant message and incident evidence in restricted records.

Automated signals assist triage only. They may miss abuse or flag benign context and cannot be the final authority for high-impact action.

## User Response

The app tells a user to stop contact, avoid forwarding or downloading abusive material, preserve their own factual context, use report/block, and contact local emergency services for immediate danger. MORT must not ask a child to investigate, confront an accused person, or repeatedly view harmful content.

## Moderator Response

1. Restrict access to a child-safety specialist and senior safety staff.
2. Assess immediate danger, ongoing contact, missing-child concern, coercion, trafficking, sexual content, and evidence-preservation needs.
3. Apply proportionate interim controls without publishing allegations.
4. Minimize staff exposure to explicit material and record every access.
5. Follow counsel-approved reporting and preservation duties, including any applicable NCMEC CyberTipline process.
6. Provide survivor-centered updates and an appeal path that does not expose another person's private evidence.

## Reporting Boundary

NCMEC operates the U.S. CyberTipline for suspected online child sexual exploitation, and immediate danger belongs with emergency services. Counsel must determine MORT's status, actual-knowledge threshold, report contents, timing, preservation, and cross-border duties before launch. Staff must use the approved company process, not personal accounts or improvised outreach.

## Privacy And Evidence

Do not expose flagged content in routine queues, notifications, screenshots, analytics, training data, or public reviews. Preserve only the scope required by policy or law. Keep restricted evidence encrypted in transit/at rest through approved providers, use short-lived grants, and maintain chain-of-custody records.

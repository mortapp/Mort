## Safety boundary

MORT provides safety controls but cannot guarantee that a participant, location, job, or interaction is safe. MORT is not emergency services, law enforcement, a background-check guarantee, an insurer, or a substitute for a trusted adult's judgment. Call local emergency services when danger may be immediate.

## Before a job

The poster must disclose the work scope, approximate area, location type, schedule, expected people present, equipment, hazards, transportation expectations, and payment preference. Exact private locations must remain restricted until the server-authorized release point. Participants must use the accepted job record and must not substitute a different person, location, schedule, or material task without the required change and confirmation flow.

Teens may use Guardian Mode, but Guardian Mode is optional unless a separate legal rule is approved for a jurisdiction. Safety tools, basic Guardian Mode, report, block, and Safety Ping must never be placed behind payment. Verification badges and sandbox records are not guarantees of conduct. Real identity collection remains disabled until an approved provider and legal review exist.

## During a job

Participants must use the server-authorized start and completion PIN flows and must never send a PIN in chat. Check-ins, Safety Ping, emergency contacts, cancellation, and report/block controls remain available. A teen may stop work and leave when they reasonably feel unsafe. No one may require secrecy, isolate a teen, introduce undisclosed people, demand unrelated transportation, request sexual or intimate conduct, supply alcohol or controlled substances, display weapons to intimidate, or pressure a teen into off-platform contact.

Location sharing, where enabled, must be purpose-limited, time-limited, visible to the user, revocable, and restricted to authorized recipients. Guardian access does not automatically include exact location or private message content.

## Evidence and incident handling

Users should provide only evidence relevant to a report. Raw identity files, private location data, and safety evidence must remain in private storage with scoped, expiring access. Staff access requires an individual account, current role, case purpose, assignment where required, and an audit event. Staff must redact unnecessary personal data and must not copy sensitive evidence to personal devices, group chats, or public reports.

Urgent child-safety, grooming, sexual-safety, abduction, violence, scam, prohibited-job, identity, and retaliation signals require priority triage by appropriately assigned humans. Automated classifiers may route or prioritize a case but may not determine guilt, ban a user, reveal evidence, or contact authorities on their own.

## Enforcement and appeal

MORT may restrict communication, remove content or a job, suspend an account, preserve records, or ban an account when an authorized reviewer records the reason and audit trail. High-impact actions require specialized authority. A ban may not be reversed through the ordinary account-status control; reversal requires an independent appeal reviewer who was not the original banning actor.

MORT does not claim staffed 24/7 coverage. Response and escalation targets remain operational targets, not emergency guarantees. Public marketplace activation remains blocked until approved legal versions, owner approval, provider readiness, and accepted moderation staffing are recorded.

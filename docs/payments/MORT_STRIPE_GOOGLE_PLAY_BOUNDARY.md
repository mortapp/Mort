# Stripe and Google Play Boundary

Google Play Billing covers optional Android digital app benefits. Stripe covers real-world marketplace job obligations and connected-account money movement. A Play product cannot fund a job, pay a worker, cash out, purchase currency convertible to money, or settle a dispute. A Stripe payment cannot unlock MORT Plus, ad-free, profile styles, username credits, or job-boost digital entitlements inside Android.

The schemas, provider functions, entitlement checks, receipt tokens, webhooks, refunds, and reporting remain separate. Cross-provider identifiers are never treated as interchangeable. Product and policy changes require a new Play payments-policy review.

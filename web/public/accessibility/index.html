Products covered

This statement covers the public MORT website, the MORT iOS application, and the MORT Android application. MORT is committed to making its website and mobile applications accessible and usable for as many people as possible.

Current support

MORT is designed for screen readers (VoiceOver on iOS, TalkBack on Android, and screen readers on web), scalable text, keyboard navigation on web, visible focus, dark mode, reduced motion, descriptive labels, and permission-denial alternatives. Physical VoiceOver, TalkBack, and device testing remain required before release; MORT does not claim a specific WCAG conformance level at this time.

Alternatives

Location features include approximate and manual-area paths. Safety and account controls must not depend on color alone. Support can help with an accessible route but cannot bypass identity, eligibility, or safety rules.

Known limitations

Physical assistive-technology device testing across the full iOS and Android app surface is ongoing and not yet complete. Some screens may not yet meet the intended standard; please report specific barriers using the contact below.

Feedback

Report accessibility barriers to mortapp@googlegroups.com. Include the platform (website, iOS, or Android), device, OS version, page or screen, assistive technology used if any, and what you expected, without sending passwords or sensitive incident evidence.

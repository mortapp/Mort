# MORT Document Review Limitations

Status: required disclosure draft. Real document collection remains disabled.

## What visual review cannot establish alone

- That a document is authentic or unaltered
- That the issuer's record remains valid
- That the account holder is the legal person named
- That a selfie, device biometric, or login reveals legal identity
- That a school affiliation is government identity
- That a public-record match proves account ownership
- That a reviewed person will act safely, pay, perform work, or follow policy

## Precise labels

Allowed labels include Email ownership confirmed, Phone ownership confirmed, School affiliation confirmed, Partner organization confirmed, MORT document reviewed, Age evidence reviewed, Business registration matched, Government digital credential verified, and Provider-backed identity verified.

Do not collapse these into a vague `Verified` badge. Each public label needs a user-readable explanation of what was and was not established, source type, recency where relevant, and revocation/expiration behavior.

## Operational limits

Human review introduces error, bias, coercion, accessibility barriers, and breach risk. Alternative evidence routes require written standards and trained staff. Minimize evidence, prohibit downloads by default, and keep consequential decisions appealable.

## Current limitation

No trained review operation, legal/privacy approval, live retention process, breach-response staffing, production monitoring, or public collection authorization has been established by the codebase. The foundation is not a working identity provider.

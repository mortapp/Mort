# MORT Optional Guardian Value

Guardian Mode is optional by default. It is a support relationship, not an invisible monitoring system and not a measure of teen trustworthiness.

## Useful Scope

- Voluntary invitation, acceptance, and unlinking
- Teen-visible sharing boundaries
- Guardian-selected notification categories, digest timing, and quiet hours
- Selected accepted-job summaries or check-ins when the teen shares them
- Safety Ping alerts under the linked policy
- Voluntary transport planning and emergency contact context
- Plain-language guidance for discussing safe work expectations

## Free Baseline

Basic Guardian Mode and safety alerts remain free. Guardian Plus may add optional organization or convenience only after real RevenueCat configuration; it cannot unlock private messages, exact location, proof, or unrelated work details.

## Prohibited Uses

Do not require a guardian for normal browsing, applying, accepted-participant messaging, or completion unless a reviewed jurisdictional or job-specific policy genuinely requires it. Do not use guardian participation in ranking, verification, enforcement, or monetization pressure. Legal review must define age/jurisdiction exceptions before launch.

# MORT Outage Communication Templates

Replace bracketed fields only with approved, verified facts. Do not identify a
minor, expose safety details, speculate about attackers, promise dispatch, or
publish legal conclusions.

## Investigating

> MORT is investigating an issue affecting [capability] that began around
> [time and timezone]. We have limited the affected feature while we verify the
> impact. Core safety tools are [available/unavailable]. For immediate danger,
> contact local emergency services. Next update: [time].

## Service Restored

> MORT restored [capability] at [time and timezone]. We are monitoring the
> service and reviewing what happened. [Specific user action, if verified]. We
> will share further information through the approved support/privacy channel.

## Security/Privacy Notice Holding Statement

> MORT is investigating a potential security or privacy issue. We contained
> [narrow capability] and are working with our security, privacy, safety, and
> legal reviewers. We will contact affected people through verified channels if
> notification is required. Do not send passwords, IDs, exact locations, or
> evidence by ordinary email or chat.

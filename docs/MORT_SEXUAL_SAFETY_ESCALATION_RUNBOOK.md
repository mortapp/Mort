# MORT Sexual Safety Escalation Runbook

Status: operational draft. Do not use with real users until trained staff, counsel, on-call ownership, secure reporting credentials, and escalation contacts are approved.

## Immediate Intake

1. Create or locate the restricted incident case.
2. Determine whether anyone is in immediate danger, missing, currently traveling to a meeting, or unable to leave.
3. Show emergency-service guidance for immediate danger. MORT is not an emergency dispatcher.
4. Stop ordinary contact through block/thread controls when appropriate; do not alert an accused person in a way that increases danger.
5. Preserve the relevant message, report, attachment metadata, account, job, application, and timeline scope.

## Severity Routing

- Critical: apparent child sexual abuse material, active enticement, trafficking, abduction, sextortion, immediate meetup danger, assault, or credible threat. Page child-safety specialist and senior safety moderator immediately; involve legal/reporting reviewer.
- High: repeated sexual harassment, grooming progression, stalking, secrecy pressure, identity mismatch, or unwanted sexual imagery. Restrict contact and prioritize same-shift trained review.
- Moderate: ambiguous isolated sexualized language or boundary issue without immediate danger. Preserve bounded context and review promptly.

## Evidence Handling

Use metadata-first review. A reviewer must state a reason before a short-lived evidence grant. Do not download to personal devices, forward through ordinary email/chat, copy into tickets, or create extra screenshots. Record access, decision, preservation, and transfer events.

## Legal And External Reporting

Only designated staff use the counsel-approved NCMEC, law-enforcement, emergency-disclosure, or regulator process. Validate recipient identity and authority, disclose only the approved scope, and record request/reference, reviewer, legal basis, transfer method, and time. Do not promise a user that a report will cause arrest or immediate intervention.

## User Care

- Use non-blaming language and do not require repeated retelling.
- Explain account controls, case status, evidence limits, and available appeal/support paths.
- Do not require Guardian Mode. Offer the user's chosen Safety Circle or trusted-contact option when safe.
- Avoid sharing report details with guardians beyond the teen's granted permissions unless counsel approves a necessary emergency disclosure.

## Closure

Record the public-safe status, restricted findings, actions, external references, preservation status, follow-up owner, appeal route, and next review date. Resolve only when immediate safeguards, reporting duties, and follow-up ownership are addressed. Closing a case does not erase preserved evidence or imply that a person is safe.

## Prelaunch Drill

Run tabletop exercises for explicit-image detection, false positive, sextortion, person mismatch, abduction concern, reporter unavailable, provider outage, unlawful request, insider access, evidence leak, and after-hours escalation. Document failures and rerun before launch.

1. You must host this file on your public domain at `https://[your-domain.com]/app-ads.txt`.
2. Ensure the marketing URL submitted to the App Store Connect points to a page on `[your-domain.com]`.
3. AdMob will automatically crawl the marketing URL domain to find the `app-ads.txt` file.
4. Until AdMob successfully crawls and verifies this file, real ad fill rates will be significantly reduced or zero.

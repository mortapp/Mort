# MORT Abduction Concern Runbook

> **DRAFT — NOT ATTORNEY REVIEWED OR LEGALLY APPROVED**

## Trigger

A report suggests force, coercion, confinement, transportation against will, grooming-linked removal, or another possible abduction.

## Actions

1. Treat possible immediate danger as an emergency and direct the reporter to emergency services.
2. Do not contact or warn the suspected person. Do not tell staff to recover the teen.
3. Apply emergency preservation to relevant job, participant, location, check-in, message, and access records.
4. Restrict suspected accounts only as needed for containment and preserve appeal records.
5. Route law-enforcement requests through the request runbook and qualified reviewer.
6. Limit internal discussion to assigned responders; no group-chat screenshots or public statements.

## Boundaries

Staff preserve facts, protect affected people, and use qualified escalation. They do not confront a suspected offender, promise secrecy they cannot keep, conduct amateur criminal investigations, give individualized legal advice, publish accusations, or represent that this draft is legally approved. Every access and disclosure is minimized and logged.

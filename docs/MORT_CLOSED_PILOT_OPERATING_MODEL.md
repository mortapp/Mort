# MORT Closed Pilot Operating Model

Status: operational draft. The hosted backend enforces closed-pilot gates, but no public launch is authorized by this document.

## Boundary

The active mode is `closed-organization-supported-pilot`. Unrestricted public access is false. Real identity-document collection is false. Guardian Mode remains optional.

Eligible entry sources are approved partner codes, verified partner email relationships, authorized partner staff attestations, and manually approved pilot enrollment. Codes are stored as hashes, expire, have use limits, and can be revoked. A code is an entry signal, not identity proof.

## Required operators

Before real participants are enrolled, MORT needs named adults responsible for pilot ownership, child safety, incident response, partner approval, employer review, data protection, support, and after-hours escalation. The same person must not silently approve every high-impact verification decision.

## Cohort flow

1. MORT approves a qualified organization and records environment, term, and scope.
2. Authorized staff receive only the minimum partner permissions needed.
3. The participant enters through an approved route and completes current training, rules, and consent acknowledgements.
4. An authorized operator reviews enrollment and records the decision and reason.
5. Adults and businesses satisfy additional email, phone, training, organization, and manual approval requirements.
6. Each job receives a server-owned location and prohibited-work decision before it can be visible or actionable.
7. Incidents, partner changes, and revocations are audited and can suspend access without exposing participant vulnerability.

## Data separation

Partner staff may see only participants connected to their organization. They cannot read unrestricted messages, passwords, private resource bookmarks, earnings, full location history, or document-vault evidence. Job posters cannot see partner, school, shelter, housing, counselor, or family information about a teen.

## Pause conditions

Pause enrollment or job activity for an unstaffed incident queue, failed RLS/storage regression, unreviewed partner access, missing mandated-reporting protocol, material privacy incident, unclear youth-labor compliance, or any job category that cannot be safely reviewed.

## Exit and evaluation

At cohort end, revoke temporary staff and invite access, close expired enrollments, complete required incident follow-up, apply retention schedules, and produce de-identified outcome and harm findings. Do not turn a pilot account into unrestricted public access automatically.

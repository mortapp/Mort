# MORT Adult And Business Value Proposition

## Core Promise

Define a safe local need clearly, find an appropriate teen applicant, coordinate through participant-only tools, and close the work with accountable history without running a heavyweight hiring system.

## Practical Value

- Fast job briefs and age-aware templates reduce missing expectations.
- Applicant eligibility, structured proposals, availability, verification context, and status history support a better decision without secret scoring.
- Scheduling, change handling, cancellation recovery, and messaging reduce coordination overhead.
- Private proof review supports jobs that legitimately need evidence without making teen media public.
- Reviews, trusted-worker lists, repeat invitations, reusable requirements, and recurring schedules reduce repeated hiring effort.
- Business verification and team permissions can add accountability after policy and operations review.

## Optional Paid Value

Adult Pro, job boosts, and related perks may offer organization, visibility, or convenience only when RevenueCat/App Store configuration supplies the real product and localized price. Paying must not bypass safety ranking, reveal private data, force a teen response, or remove the free hiring path.

MORT does not guarantee worker availability, work quality, legal classification, safety, earnings, or insurance coverage.

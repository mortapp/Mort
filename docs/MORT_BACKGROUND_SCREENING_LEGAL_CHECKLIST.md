# MORT Background Screening Legal Checklist

Status: preparation only. Background screening is not enabled and no screening badge is issued. Licensed counsel must determine whether and how employment, marketplace, youth, consumer-reporting, discrimination, privacy, and local laws apply.

## Launch Decision

- Identify whether MORT, the job poster, or both make an employment or eligibility decision.
- Determine whether a third-party report is a consumer report and whether the provider is a consumer reporting agency.
- Define a permissible purpose for every report and prohibit reuse for another purpose.
- Map federal, state, county, and city restrictions, including fair-chance timing, individualized assessment, salary/credit limits, and required notices.
- Decide whether screening minors is prohibited, inappropriate, or requires a separate legal basis. The current design prepares enhanced adult screening only.

## Provider Due Diligence

- Contract for lawful data sources, reasonable accuracy procedures, dispute handling, correction/deletion, security, breach notice, retention, audit rights, and subprocessor controls.
- Confirm the provider supplies its legal identity, address, phone, report copy, and current FCRA rights summary where applicable.
- Test name collisions, aliases, expunged/sealed records, incomplete disposition, stale data, and common-identity errors.
- Prohibit random public-record scraping and opaque risk scores.

## Before Procurement

- Present any legally required disclosure in a clear, conspicuous, stand-alone format.
- Keep unrelated waivers, releases, Terms, safety language, and marketing out of the stand-alone disclosure.
- Obtain affirmative written authorization and retain its version, timestamp, purpose, and subject.
- Provide any investigative-report notice and nature/scope description required for the selected report.
- Certify required notice, authorization, permissible purpose, nondiscrimination, and compliance statements to the provider.

## Decision Controls

- Apply the same job-related policy consistently and review disparate-impact risk.
- Require human review; do not let a score or database hit make a final decision.
- Verify identity match, disposition, recency, relevance, job relationship, rehabilitation/context, and applicable exclusions.
- Do not represent a screening result as a guarantee of safety.

## Adverse Action And Disputes

- Before a covered adverse action, provide the report relied on and the current Summary of Rights, then allow a legally sufficient review/dispute period.
- Pause the decision while a timely accuracy dispute is investigated when required by policy or law.
- After a final adverse action, provide the required decision notice, provider contact details, provider non-decision statement, free-copy right, and dispute right.
- Store notice versions, delivery evidence, dates, decision basis, and reviewer identity in a restricted audit record.

## Security And Disposal

- Keep raw reports out of Swift, Flutter, public tables, client logs, analytics, and notifications.
- Use restricted server storage, purpose-bound grants, least-privilege roles, and sensitive-access logs.
- Apply an approved retention schedule and secure disposal process when reports are no longer needed.

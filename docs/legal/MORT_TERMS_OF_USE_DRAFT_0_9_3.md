# MORT Terms of Use Draft 0.9.3

## Account and marketplace use

Users must provide accurate age and role information, protect sign-in credentials, follow safety controls, and use only their own account. MORT is limited to users age 13 or older. Closed-pilot and account-eligibility controls may prevent a user from applying, posting, starting, or completing a job.

Adults fund an accepted Stripe job before work begins. MORT does not guarantee work quality, a job, payment eligibility, or a particular dispute outcome. Funds are pending release, not escrow, and payment release can be delayed during a dispute.

Start and finish PINs record in-person actions but do not guarantee identity or quality. Adult cancellation after work begins can lead to partial teen compensation after review. A non-safety abandonment may lead to no or partial payment after review; safety cancellation must not create an automatic penalty.

## Support and records

Evidence does not guarantee an outcome. MORT may review relevant MORT records and authorized evidence under the accepted policy. Platform decisions are not court judgments or criminal findings, and appeals may be available.

Stripe eligibility and any provider-required guardian process apply independently from optional Guardian Mode. Support and AI-assisted help are not emergency, medical, or legal services. Email is an unlinked manual fallback and must not contain secrets or sensitive identity documents.

Support retention differs from evidence preservation during an active dispute. Any material term update requires versioning and reacceptance where required. This document is an inactive legal draft.

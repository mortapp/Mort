# Cash App Limitations

Cash App Pay, where Stripe offers it, is a customer payment method for an eligible PaymentIntent. It is not the same as paying a teen's Cash App account, a connected-account payout destination, escrow, or proof that a worker received funds.

MORT must rely on Stripe's account/country/device eligibility and PaymentSheet-returned methods. It must not advertise Cash App unless it appears in the approved provider configuration and is tested. External Cash App payment preference remains outside MORT processing and carries no Stripe-funded/refund/dispute protection from MORT.

Public policy & safety

Support

Account, privacy, and safety support routes for MORT users and reviewers.

Current limits: MORT is a 13+ local-work coordination service with account eligibility restrictions. It does not guarantee identity, safety, jobs, or payment; it does not process real-world job payments; and identity verification is not currently available.

Account-linked help

Signed-in users should use the in-app Support Center so requests carry authorized account context.

Other routes

Delete an account through account deletion. Review safety guidance in the Safety Center. For urgent danger, contact local emergency services.

# MORT Adverse Action Process Draft

Status: legal and operational draft. Not enabled. Counsel must determine whether MORT or a job poster is the decision maker and which FCRA, fair-chance, employment, marketplace, and local requirements apply.

## Trigger

Use this process only when a covered consumer report may contribute in whole or in part to a less favorable employment or eligibility decision. Safety restrictions based on independent conduct reports follow the incident process, not a mislabeled screening adverse-action shortcut.

## Pre-Adverse Action

1. Freeze the proposed decision as `pre_adverse_review`; do not publish a rejection badge.
2. Have an authorized human verify person match, report source, disposition, date, policy relevance, and jurisdiction rules.
3. Deliver the complete report relied on and the current Summary of Rights through an approved secure channel.
4. Identify the proposed action and explain that no final decision has been made.
5. Provide provider contact and dispute instructions.
6. Record delivery method, timestamp, document versions, reviewer, and response deadline.
7. Allow the legally sufficient period for review, explanation, identity correction, or provider dispute.

## Dispute Hold

When the person identifies an accuracy problem, pause the final action where required and mark `dispute_pending`. Do not ask the person to prove a database error solely through public uploads. Route the dispute to the provider, preserve communications, accept contextual information, and refresh the report after correction when authorized.

## Final Decision

An authorized human considers the corrected report, user response, job relationship, consistency, and approved policy. If the adverse action proceeds, deliver a final notice containing every required item, including the report provider's contact details, the provider non-decision statement, and applicable free-copy and dispute rights. If it does not proceed, close the proposal without a public accusation.

## Audit And Privacy

Record the decision basis, policy version, notice versions, delivery proof, timestamps, reviewer, and appeal path. Keep raw reports and sensitive notes restricted. Public profiles must not reveal criminal-history allegations, dispute details, or adverse-action reasons.

## Prohibited Automation

No automated score, model, or raw record hit may send a final adverse-action notice, reject a user, or create a public badge without human review and the approved legal process.

# MORT AI Support Disclosure Draft 0.9.3

## Automated support

FAQ-only support uses approved MORT help content and does not require an external model. Optional external AI generation remains server-controlled, authenticated, filtered, rate-limited, and disabled without approved credentials. Automated help is clearly separate from human review.

AI must not receive evidence files, passwords, codes, payment tokens, exact addresses, government IDs, or unnecessary private messages. It does not determine evidence truth, fraud, legal status, punishment, charges, transfers, refunds, or dispute outcomes. It must not claim a human reviewed a case when no server record proves that.

## Product boundaries

Adults fund accepted Stripe jobs before work starts. MORT does not guarantee work quality; disputes can delay release. Start and finish PINs record actions but are not identity guarantees. Adult after-start cancellation and non-safety abandonment may result in partial or no payment after human review. Safety cancellation cannot cause an automatic penalty.

Evidence does not guarantee an outcome. Relevant authorized records may be reviewed under policy. MORT decisions are not court judgments or criminal findings; appeals may be available. Stripe and provider guardian requirements still apply.

MORT Support and AI are not emergency, medical, or legal services and have not dispatched help. Email is an unlinked manual fallback and must not include secrets or identity documents. Support retention and dispute preservation differ. Terms require versioning and reacceptance. This draft is inactive.

# MORT Differentiation Moat

MORT's moat is not a long feature list. It is the interaction of verified local-work history, teen-aware policy, bounded family support, explainable trust, and community utility.

## Compounding Product Assets

- **Skill Passport:** verified completion, skills, reviews, references, and work habits can become more useful with each legitimate job.
- **Local Talent Bench:** adults and businesses can retain trusted workers, backup options, reusable requirements, and recurring schedules.
- **Optional Guardian boundary:** families can opt into selected check-ins and alerts without making guardian monitoring a condition of ordinary participation.
- **Explainable Opportunity Radar:** ranking follows hard safety/privacy exclusions and shows understandable match reasons instead of a hidden desirability score.
- **Community Opportunity Graph:** schools, nonprofits, events, businesses, and neighborhoods can create trusted recurring supply without turning MORT into a general social network.
- **Accountable safety operations:** free report/block/Safety Ping, restricted evidence, human decisions, audit history, and appeals improve both protection and institutional trust.

## What MORT Should Not Compete On

- Infinite-feed attention
- Cheapest-labor positioning
- Guaranteed earnings or guaranteed safety
- Mandatory guardian surveillance
- Public teen location or proof
- Opaque AI ranking
- Cosmetic badges without provenance
- Safety or basic participation behind payment

The moat only becomes real through safe marketplace density, verified outcomes, partner relationships, operational quality, and legal compliance. Code alone does not establish it.

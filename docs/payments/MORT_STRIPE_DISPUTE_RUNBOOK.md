# Stripe Dispute Runbook

1. Confirm the alert is from a signature-verified, environment-matched event and locate the payment by provider reference.
2. Freeze transfer/refund automation for the affected obligation; do not restrict unrelated safety features.
3. Assign two authorized financial reviewers and record access in the financial audit log.
4. Preserve the accepted contract version, completion confirmations, proof metadata, communication references, refunds, transfers, and provider deadline. Do not export unrelated teen data.
5. Review Stripe's evidence requirements and applicable MORT policy. Submit only necessary evidence through Stripe Dashboard.
6. Notify participants with neutral, non-accusatory status and keep reports/blocks/Safety Ping available.
7. Reconcile every provider update. On closure, record won/lost outcome, balance impact, transfer/reversal state, and follow-up restrictions.
8. Escalate suspected fraud, child safety, legal process, or data breach through the corresponding runbook; this process does not replace legal advice.

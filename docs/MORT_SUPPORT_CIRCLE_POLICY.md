# MORT Support Circle Policy

Status: implementation-aligned sharing draft. Guardian Mode remains optional.

## Teen control

A teen may enable or disable a Support Circle and invite a parent, guardian, relative, adult sibling, counselor, mentor, school liaison, social worker, shelter staff member, youth-program worker, or other trusted adult. Participation does not affect onboarding completion or independent marketplace eligibility.

## Grantable alerts

Permissions are individual and may cover Safety Ping, missed check-in, accepted-job summary, completion alert, transportation-plan alert, resource help, or organization-relationship confirmation. Alerts contain the minimum detail needed and respect Discreet Mode.

## Never granted by Support Circle

Unrestricted message reading, teen impersonation, earnings control, application changes, identity-document access, full location history, passwords, or routine approval of every job are not Support Circle permissions. A separate lawful program policy must be explicit if additional supervision is required.

## Invitations and revocation

Membership is invitation-based, status-tracked, and revocable. The teen can change routine permissions. MORT should notify the teen of permission changes and avoid exposing the nature of a trusted adult's professional role publicly.

## Emergencies

An alert is not guaranteed delivery or response. MORT does not monitor continuously or replace emergency services. Escalation copy should help the teen choose emergency services or qualified resources when immediate danger exists.

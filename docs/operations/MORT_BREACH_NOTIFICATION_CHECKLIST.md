# MORT Breach Notification Checklist

Status: technical checklist only; jurisdiction and notification decisions
require qualified legal/privacy counsel.

- [ ] Incident commander, privacy lead, security lead, child-safety lead, counsel, and scribe assigned.
- [ ] Exposure window, systems, data categories, jurisdictions, and affected population established from verified evidence.
- [ ] Minors, exact location, private messages, credentials, identity/evidence, and payment allegations evaluated separately.
- [ ] Preservation hold and chain of custody recorded with least-privilege access.
- [ ] Compromised credentials revoked and affected sessions contained.
- [ ] Applicable regulator, consumer, guardian, partner, insurer, processor, and law-enforcement duties reviewed by counsel.
- [ ] Notification content states known facts, dates, data categories, containment, user actions, contact route, and update process.
- [ ] Notification avoids victim blaming, unsafe guardian disclosure, unverified criminal labels, and operational security details.
- [ ] Delivery list independently reviewed; no bulk disclosure of recipients.
- [ ] Support capacity and child-safety escalation are staffed before notices send.
- [ ] All sent notices, approvals, timing decisions, and exceptions retained.
- [ ] Post-incident RLS/Auth/provider regression and remediation owners completed.

Delete your MORT account

Request account deletion without reinstalling the app.

Current limits: MORT is a 13+ local-work coordination service with account eligibility restrictions. It does not guarantee identity, safety, jobs, or payment; it does not process real-world job payments; and identity verification is not currently available.

What happens

After ownership verification, MORT creates an auditable request to remove the account and ordinary profile data. Narrow safety, fraud, dispute, security, evidence-preservation, and legal records may be retained with restricted access when legitimately necessary.

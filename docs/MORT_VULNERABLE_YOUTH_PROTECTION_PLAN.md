# MORT Vulnerable Youth Protection Plan

Status: safety design draft. Qualified child-safety, legal, privacy, and local program review is still required.

## Core rule

Vulnerability must improve protection without becoming a public label, advertising signal, employer filter, or reason to weaken job safety. The app does not need to know whether a teen is homeless, abused, in foster care, or in a shelter to offer no-address onboarding and private resources.

## Protective defaults

- Public profiles exclude full date of birth, legal name, residential details, school, program, shelter, counselor, housing status, student number, and partner connection.
- Discreet Mode removes sensitive notification content and job addresses.
- Support Circle sharing is teen-selected and permission-specific.
- Private resource bookmarks and Future Independence plans are owner-only under RLS.
- Unknown residential, isolated, overnight, transport-dependent, hazardous, secretive, and adult-service jobs are blocked in the pilot.
- Safety cancellation must not automatically reduce reputation.

## Immediate-danger response

Show concise emergency guidance, local emergency services, qualified crisis resources, Safety Ping, and trusted-contact options. MORT must not claim to monitor continuously, dispatch help, replace emergency services, provide legal advice, or guarantee that a contact responds.

## Human operations

A live pilot requires trained adults with defined coverage, mandated-reporting guidance applicable to the operating jurisdiction, escalation criteria, conflict handling, documentation limits, and trauma-informed communication. Do not collect a detailed abuse narrative merely to route a participant to help.

## Prohibited uses

Do not infer or expose vulnerability for ranking, monetization, engagement, ad targeting, employment selection, partner analytics, public badges, or fraud scoring. Do not tell job posters why a teen used an alternate onboarding path. Do not require a guardian link solely because a teen lacks an address or conventional documents.

## Evaluation

Test no-address, no-guardian, low-data, older-device, lost-phone, changing-contact, accessibility, literacy, language, transport, and expired-affiliation scenarios before every expansion. Exception paths may preserve access, but must not remove job-safety controls.

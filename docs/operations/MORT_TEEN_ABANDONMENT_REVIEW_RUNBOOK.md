# Teen Abandonment Review Runbook

1. Label the adult report as an allegation. The report itself creates no cooldown or reputation penalty.
2. Give the teen a response path and ask whether safety, coercion, scope change, illness, connectivity, or adult absence contributed.
3. Route any safety claim to the safety workflow and prohibit retaliation.
4. Review contract, start verification, check-ins, authorized evidence, messages, timing, and both statements.
5. Only authorized trained staff may make a final non-safety finding with reason and audit record.
6. Never apply a cooldown to a safety cancellation.
7. Notify both users neutrally and preserve appeal rights.

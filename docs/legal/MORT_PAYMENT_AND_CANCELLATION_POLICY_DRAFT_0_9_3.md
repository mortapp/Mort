# MORT Payment and Cancellation Policy Draft 0.9.3

## Funding and release

The adult reviews teen earnings, MORT's service fee, total charge, cancellation terms, partial-compensation terms, and dispute process before confirming PaymentSheet. Server-owned amounts are captured before the start PIN. The funded payment remains on the platform side pending release and is not escrow.

MORT does not guarantee work quality. Start and finish PINs record actions, not identity or quality. A finish PIN starts the configured review window. An unresolved MORT or Stripe dispute freezes normal release. Eligible release uses one idempotent server transfer; the client cannot choose an amount or destination.

## Cancellation and reviewed resolution

Before-start and after-start adult cancellation are recorded separately. After-start cancellation can result in partial teen compensation under an approved policy and human review. A confirmed non-safety teen abandonment may result in no payment or partial payment. Safety cancellation must not automatically cause a penalty.

Evidence does not guarantee an outcome. MORT may review relevant authorized records. Decisions are platform-contract decisions, not court or criminal findings, and appeals may be available. Refunds, partial refunds, transfers, and partial transfers require restricted idempotent server operations.

Stripe and connected-account eligibility apply, including possible provider guardian requirements. Support and AI help are not emergency, legal, or medical services. Email is a manual fallback and must not carry secrets or identity documents. Payment evidence may be preserved beyond normal support retention. Policy changes require versioning and reacceptance. This draft is inactive and live payment execution remains disabled.

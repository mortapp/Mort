# Legal And Teen Safety Notes

This is not legal advice. MORT needs qualified review before real users.

## Required Before Real Users

- App Store privacy nutrition labels
- Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
- teen/minor safety review
- guardian consent model review
- COPPA/state teen privacy review where applicable
- AdMob child/teen treatment review
- RevenueCat/App Store purchase disclosure review
- moderation staffing and escalation process
- incident response plan

## Monetization Constraints

- Safety cannot be paywalled.
- Job applications cannot be paywalled.
- Guardian Mode basics cannot be paywalled.
- Ads cannot appear on safety-sensitive screens.
- MORT does not process job payments, escrow, payouts, cards, or split payments.

## Flutter Rebuild Update

- Flutter legal routes are mapped for terms, privacy, community rules, payment disclaimer, verification disclaimer, ad disclosure, subscription disclosure, teen safety, and guardian guide.
- These are draft product/legal surfaces only.
- Formal legal, privacy, teen safety, labor, moderation, and App Store review remains required before real users.

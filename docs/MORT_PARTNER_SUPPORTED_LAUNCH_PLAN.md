# MORT Partner-Supported Launch Plan

Status: partner-readiness draft. It is not a partner agreement or legal approval.

## Eligible partner types

Schools, online schools, vocational programs, nonprofits, youth shelters, community centers, workforce programs, verified businesses, and local-government youth programs may be considered. Approval is not automatic based on organization type.

## Due diligence

MORT must confirm the organization, responsible representative, contact channels, program relevance, staff authorization process, youth-safety expectations, incident contacts, expiration date, and permitted attestations. Where law or program policy requires screening or background checks, completion must be documented outside participant-facing records.

## Permission model

Grant staff permissions individually and for a defined term. Available duties should be separated: participant connection, affiliation attestation, enrollment support, job sponsorship, and incident coordination. No partner permission includes unrestricted messages, earnings, identity documents, passwords, or broad participant search.

## Attestation language

Staff select only a fact they are authorized to support: appeared before partner, program affiliation, approximate age-band eligibility, program participation, staff identity, or partner relationship. Each attestation states what was not established. School or program affiliation never becomes Government identity verified.

## Launch sequence

1. Approve a small number of organizations with named owners.
2. Train staff using documented scenarios and privacy rules.
3. Issue expiring, limited-use invitations in the correct environment.
4. Enroll a bounded cohort and approve only reviewed low-risk jobs.
5. Hold weekly safety and access reviews with partner escalation contacts.
6. Revoke access promptly when staff, authority, or affiliation changes.
7. Evaluate outcomes and harms before expanding organizations, geography, or job categories.

## Partner promises MORT must not make

MORT must not promise guaranteed income, guaranteed safety, authoritative identity from visual review, crisis-response replacement, current resource availability, payment protection, or unrestricted future access.

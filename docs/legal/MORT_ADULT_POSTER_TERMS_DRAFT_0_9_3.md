# MORT Adult Poster Terms Draft 0.9.3

## Adult responsibilities

An adult poster must accurately describe the work, location type, schedule, hazards, people present, equipment, earnings, fees, cancellation terms, and completion requirements. The adult must not request prohibited work, off-scope work, identity documents, public PIN sharing, or unsafe contact.

The adult funds the exact accepted job through PaymentSheet before a start PIN can be generated. MORT does not guarantee teen performance or work quality. A funded payment remains pending release and is not escrow. A dispute freezes ordinary release.

The adult exchanges start and finish PINs in person. PINs record handoff actions only; they are not identity or quality guarantees. Refusing a finish PIN does not create an automatic refund. Cancellation after work begins can result in reviewed partial teen compensation.

## Review boundaries

A teen's non-safety abandonment may result in no or partial payment after human review. A safety-related cancellation must not automatically cause a penalty. Evidence does not guarantee an outcome, and MORT may review only relevant authorized records under policy. MORT decisions are platform-contract decisions, not court judgments or criminal findings; appeals may be available.

Stripe, connected-account, and provider guardian requirements remain controlling. Support is not emergency, legal, or medical assistance. Email is a manual fallback and must not include credentials, card data, addresses, or identity documents. Retention differs for ordinary support and preserved disputes. New terms require controlled versioning and reacceptance. This draft is inactive.

# MORT Homeless and Housing-Insecure Youth Access Policy

Status: privacy and access draft. The product intentionally does not diagnose or publicly label housing status.

## Access without disclosure

A teen may use `city_state`, `partner_supported`, or `location_deferred` onboarding. The latter two modes allow eligible pilot onboarding without city and state. MORT stores the setup mode, not a reason or housing condition.

MORT does not universally require a permanent address, parent-linked account, traditional school ID, government ID, private bedroom, conventional family arrangement, personal vehicle, bank account, or stable phone plan.

## Alternatives

Approved alternatives may include a school or program email, expiring partner code, authorized school liaison or youth-program attestation, vocational affiliation, voluntarily supplied government evidence when collection is operationally enabled, or a documented manual exception. Current real-document collection is disabled.

## Privacy boundary

Housing, shelter, foster, abuse, family, counselor, school, and program circumstances are not public fields. Posters, other teens, guardians, ordinary admins, advertisers, and ranking systems must not receive these details. Partner staff see only scoped organization connections.

## Job location

No-address onboarding does not remove job-location safety. Pilot jobs still require an approved staffed, public, visible, or organization-supported location. Exact meeting details should be disclosed only at the appropriate stage and only to authorized participants.

## Support language

Do not ask a participant to prove homelessness to unlock basic safety or marketplace eligibility. Do not provide runaway instructions. Offer private reviewed resources, lawful adulthood preparation, trusted support choices, and emergency guidance without claiming availability or replacing qualified services.

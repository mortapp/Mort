Legal and safety center

Public privacy, safety, support, accessibility, and account-control information for MORT.

Current limits: MORT is a 13+ local-work coordination service with account eligibility restrictions. It does not guarantee identity, safety, jobs, or payment; it does not process real-world job payments; and identity verification is not currently available.

What MORT is today

MORT is a 13+ service for local work coordination. Public visitors can learn about the mission and safety model, but jobs, participant messaging, private locations, contracts, and active-job workflows require server-approved account eligibility.

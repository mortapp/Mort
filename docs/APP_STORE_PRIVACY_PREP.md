# App Store Privacy Preparation

Prepare final disclosures for account data, DOB/age gating, approximate location, messages, reports, proof/verification uploads, guardian links, notifications, purchase records, ads, moderation/AI events, and progression activity. Progression activity includes XP events, level, rank, badges, Safety Streak, Motion Token balance and cosmetic ownership, goals derived from those events, and the teen's optional leaderboard preference. These records support app functionality and do not add cross-app tracking.

Before submission:

- Publish final privacy policy, terms, community rules, safety policy, payment disclaimer, and deletion process.
- Complete Apple privacy nutrition labels from actual production data collection.
- Confirm retention/deletion periods and guardian privacy boundaries.
- Verify camera, photo, and notification purpose strings against shipped behavior.
- Keep `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` aligned with the server-stored product interaction data and continue declaring `NSPrivacyTracking=false` unless the shipped SDK configuration changes.
- Document that MORT does not process job payments or provide escrow.
- Complete legal, privacy, child/teen-safety, and App Review review.

This document is preparation, not legal certification.

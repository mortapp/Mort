# MORT External Image Processing Privacy Review

> **DRAFT — NOT ATTORNEY REVIEWED OR LEGALLY APPROVED**

External processing of a minor's identity document or face is prohibited until all items below are approved and evidenced:

- licensed privacy and biometric counsel review
- child-safety and youth-consent review
- explicit, age-appropriate disclosure and any required consent
- vendor agreement, data-processing terms, subprocessor list, and breach duties
- permitted purpose, region, transfer mechanism, and retention schedule
- server-only credentials and egress allowlist
- temporary derivative or redaction where technically possible
- deletion after response and verified provider deletion terms
- malicious URL handling and reviewer-only result access
- accessibility alternative, appeal, and nondiscrimination review
- operational readiness, incident response, insurance, and audit approval

No raw image or provider URL is returned to Swift or Flutter. No provider result independently establishes authenticity or legal identity. Current status: **DISABLED; SYNTHETIC QA ONLY**.

# MORT Review Appeal Process

Status: process draft. A staffed appeal operation does not yet exist.

## Notice

A consequential decision notice should state the precise outcome, what it affects, what was not established, a useful reason category, expiration where relevant, and how to appeal. Do not expose detection rules or accuse the user of a crime without a lawful basis and qualified process.

## Submission

The subject may appeal within the documented window using an accessible channel. An appeal should require only the information needed to challenge the decision. Never request identity documents through ordinary chat, email, or a support-ticket attachment.

## Independence

An appeal reviewer must not be reviewer A or reviewer B for the original high-impact decision and must pass a conflict check. Access remains case-scoped, reason-required, short-lived, and audited.

## Outcomes

The reviewer may uphold, modify, reverse, expire, or request narrowly defined additional information. The outcome records reasons, timestamps, evidence limitations, retention effect, and marketplace effect. Revoking one trust signal should not erase unrelated valid signals.

## Safety and timing

Appeal-safe retention may temporarily pause deletion only under written policy. Emergency safety restrictions may remain while an appeal is pending, but should be no broader or longer than necessary. Publish service targets only after adequate staffing exists.

# MORT Missing Person Escalation

> **DRAFT — NOT ATTORNEY REVIEWED OR LEGALLY APPROVED**

## Trigger

A teen is unexpectedly unreachable, misses a required check-in, or an authorized contact raises a credible concern.

## Immediate actions

1. If danger may be immediate, direct the reporter to emergency services; do not impose a waiting period.
2. Preserve account, job, location-release, check-in, message, and incident metadata without altering source records.
3. Notify the incident manager and only authorized safety contacts according to the teen's permissions and emergency policy.
4. Do not ask volunteers to search private property or contact a suspected person.
5. Record times, factual statements, actions, and lawful-request references.

## Closure

Confirm safety through an authorized source, document the basis, remove temporary access when no longer needed, offer support and appeal, and conduct a privacy-preserving review.

## Boundaries

Staff preserve facts, protect affected people, and use qualified escalation. They do not confront a suspected offender, promise secrecy they cannot keep, conduct amateur criminal investigations, give individualized legal advice, publish accusations, or represent that this draft is legally approved. Every access and disclosure is minimized and logged.

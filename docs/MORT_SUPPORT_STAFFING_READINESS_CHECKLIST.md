# MORT Support Staffing Readiness Checklist

Updated: 2026-08-01

Status: external gate, not staffed

The code-controlled queue and authorization system is present. No item below
may be checked merely because the software exists.

## Ownership

- [ ] A named Support operations owner has accepted responsibility.
- [ ] A named trust-and-safety owner has accepted urgent-case responsibility.
- [ ] A named privacy owner has accepted deletion/privacy-case responsibility.
- [ ] A named child-safety/CSAM point of contact has accepted Play Console and
  lawful escalation responsibility.
- [ ] A named identity-review owner has accepted provider exception, retention,
  discrimination, and appeal responsibility before provider activation.
- [ ] A named financial-operations owner has accepted dispute, refund,
  reconciliation, tax, and retention responsibility before payment activation.
- [ ] A named legal/compliance owner coordinates licensed counsel decisions and
  document-version approval without acting as counsel unless qualified.
- [ ] A named engineering incident owner and backup are available.
- [ ] Coverage hours, holidays, and handoff ownership are approved and truthful.
- [ ] Published response targets are supported by measured staffing capacity.
- [ ] `targets_are_commitments` remains false until those commitments exist.

## People And Access

- [ ] Every staff member has an individual verified account and least-privilege
  role assignment with an expiry.
- [ ] Support agents, managers, safety reviewers, and privacy reviewers are
  separated according to responsibility.
- [ ] Moderators, ban-appeal reviewers, identity reviewers, financial operators,
  child-safety specialists, and incident responders are separated where a
  conflict or high-impact action exists.
- [ ] No generic admin account relies on broad Support access.
- [ ] Joiner, role-change, suspension, and departure procedures are rehearsed.
- [ ] Staff training covers minors, grooming, self-harm language, evidence,
  privacy, job PIN secrecy, emergency limitations, and mandatory escalation.
- [ ] Background-check and employment decisions have legal approval where
  required; MORT software does not make those decisions.

## Operations

- [ ] Queue ownership and backup coverage have been tested with real staff.
- [ ] Urgent escalation drills, missed-response alerts, and manager review have
  been timed and accepted.
- [ ] Appeal independence and conflict-of-interest handling are documented.
- [ ] Ban appeals are assigned to someone other than the original banning actor,
  and assignment expiry/reassignment has been rehearsed.
- [ ] Job/review enforcement training covers allowed reason codes, neutral notes,
  access logging, proportional actions, and prohibited inference of guilt.
- [ ] Evidence access, retention, legal hold, and deletion rules are approved.
- [ ] Child-safety staff know never to request or move suspected CSAM through
  ordinary support/email channels and have counsel-approved escalation steps.
- [ ] Identity and financial queues remain unavailable until their production
  providers, procedures, insurance, and staffing gates are approved.
- [ ] Privacy-safe metrics are reviewed without exporting raw case content.
- [ ] Incident communications and regulator/law-enforcement response rules have
  been approved by counsel.

## Launch Decision

- [ ] Legal, privacy, child-safety, and marketplace owners signed the pilot
  decision.
- [ ] Public support copy matches actual hours and response capability.
- [ ] Named support, privacy, and child-safety contacts are live, monitored, and
  entered accurately in public pages and Play Console.
- [ ] Moderation staffing and incident on-call controls have real recorded owner
  approval; software existence alone is not accepted as evidence.
- [ ] The public marketplace remains closed until staffing and production
  identity verification are both established.
- [ ] A rollback/closure decision-maker is named and reachable.

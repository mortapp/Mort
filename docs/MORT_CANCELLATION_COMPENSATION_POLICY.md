# MORT Cancellation and Compensation Policy

Status: engineering policy and legal draft; not attorney-approved.

- Before start, cancellation closes or withdraws the workflow when no funded obligation exists.
- After funding or work start, cancellation opens a human-reviewed dispute/support record.
- The server calculates a policy recommendation from the accepted obligation and versioned basis-point control; Flutter cannot choose an amount.
- A recommendation does not move money and does not guarantee payment.
- Safety-related exits are routed separately and do not receive automatic reputation penalties or abandonment cooldowns.
- Adult refusal of the finish PIN opens a completion evidence review.
- An adult abandonment allegation alone does not penalize a teen. The teen can respond and identify safety circumstances before any separate authorized decision.

Provider execution remains disabled. Qualified legal, labor, tax, and minor-safety review must approve the final policy before real users.

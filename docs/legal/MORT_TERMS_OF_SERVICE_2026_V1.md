# MORT — MASTER LEGAL TERMS & DEFENSE ARCHITECTURE

**Document Ref:** MORT-LEGAL-2026-V1
**Jurisdiction:** United States / Indiana
**Effective Date stated in source:** October 2026

## Operational Architecture Disclaimer

MORT ("Platform") is operated strictly as a peer-to-peer venue software and communications infrastructure connecting independent individuals. MORT is not an employer, staffing agency, employment agency, or labor broker. Payment processing services are facilitated directly through Stripe Connect in accordance with its marketplace terms.

## 1. Platform Classification & Independence Defense

### 1.1 Marketplace Venue Only
MORT provides software infrastructure enabling teens ("Youth Workers", ages 13–17) and local individuals or businesses ("Posters") to discover, schedule, and confirm completion of localized casual tasks. MORT does not hire, employ, direct, control, or supervise Youth Workers, nor does it guarantee or assign work.

### 1.2 Absence of Employment Control
In accordance with state and federal labor standards (including the Fair Labor Standards Act), MORT exercises zero control over the execution, tools, methods, or timing of tasks. Posters and Youth Workers retain exclusive authority over task parameters, safety determinations, and payment authorization. Nothing on MORT creates an employer-employee relationship, agency, joint venture, or partnership.

## 2. Enforceable Parental Consent & Liability Shield

### 2.1 Verified Parental Consent
Before any Youth Worker account is activated, MORT requires verifiable parental/guardian consent in compliance with state minor protection guidelines. The Legal Guardian ("Guardian") authorizes the minor’s participation on the Platform, confirms agreement to these Terms, and retains oversight rights over account activity.

### 2.2 Parental Guaranty & Indemnification
By consenting to a Youth Worker account, the Guardian affirmatively assumes full liability, financial responsibility, and legal accountability for all actions, omissions, conduct, and agreements initiated by the Youth Worker on MORT. The Guardian agrees to defend and indemnify MORT against any claims brought by or on behalf of the minor.

## 3. Safety Disclaimer & Limitation of Assumption of Duty

### Non-Guaranty of Third-Party Conduct
Identity verification, PIN-based check-ins, location pings, and safety tools provided by MORT are administrative risk-reduction mechanisms only. The provision of these software features DOES NOT constitute an assumption of duty by MORT to guarantee absolute physical safety, job site compliance, or non-negligence of third parties.

### 3.1 Verification Disclaimers
User identity verification services and background checks (where applicable) rely on third-party databases. MORT makes no express or implied warranties regarding the absolute accuracy, completeness, or real-time currency of any user credentials or profile assertions.

## 4. Prohibited Tasks & Child Labor Compliance

### 4.1 Statutory Safety Boundaries
MORT strictly prohibits the posting, offering, or execution of hazardous tasks, as defined under state statutory provisions (e.g., Indiana Code § 22-2-18.1) and federal hazardous occupation orders. Users violating these boundaries face immediate permanent platform banning and potential referral to authorities.

## 5. Mandatory Binding Arbitration & Dispute Resolution

### 5.1 Dispute Protocol
In the event of any controversy, claim, or dispute arising out of or relating to the use of MORT, the parties agree to first attempt informal resolution by written notice to MORT Legal. If unresolved within 30 days, the claim shall be settled exclusively by individual binding arbitration.

### 5.2 Class Action and Jury Trial Waiver
ALL USERS AND GUARDIANS EXPRESSLY AGREE THAT CLAIMS MAY ONLY BE BROUGHT IN AN INDIVIDUAL CAPACITY AND NOT AS A PLAINTIFF OR CLASS MEMBER IN ANY PURPORTED CLASS, CONSOLIDATED, OR REPRESENTATIVE PROCEEDING. THE PARTIES IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY LEGAL PROCEEDING.

## 6. Marketplace Payments & Financial Settlement

### Stripe Connect Integration & Payment Processing
Payment processing for MORT marketplace transactions is provided by Stripe, Inc. pursuant to the Stripe Connected Account Agreement. MORT is not an escrow agent, fiduciary, bank, or money transmitter. By using MORT, Users and Guardians agree to Stripe's terms of service and authorize MORT to facilitate platform payment holds and transfers via Stripe Connect upon task completion.

### 6.1 Payment Authorization Holds & Settlement
When a Poster books or confirms a task, payment amounts are collected or authorized via standard Stripe marketplace holds ("Platform Authorization Holds"). Funds are disbursed to the Youth Worker's connected account upon mutual task completion confirmation or platform dispute resolution. MORT incurs zero liability for third-party bank delays, payment processing failures, or statutory tax reporting obligations.

## 7. Privacy Architecture & Minor Protection

### 7.1 Minimal Data Footprint & Messaging Privacy
MORT collects only necessary telemetry required to facilitate connection, secure processing, and safety. In compliance with minor privacy standards, end-to-end messaging between Users and Youth Workers is protected; message contents are not accessible by Guardians or third parties except under automated safety filtering or valid legal process.

### 7.2 Guardian Safety Controls
Guardians maintain administrative controls over connected minor accounts, including viewing active task status, safety check-in status, connected payout accounts, and requesting immediate account suspension or data deletion.

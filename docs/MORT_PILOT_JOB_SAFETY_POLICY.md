# MORT Pilot Job Safety Policy

Status: implementation-aligned pilot policy. Youth labor, licensing, insurance, accessibility, and local legal review are required before live work.

## Allowed starting conditions

Pilot jobs are limited to low-risk work in verified businesses, schools, nonprofits, community centers, staffed community projects, public events, or visible outdoor community spaces. Organization approval alone does not make a task safe.

## Initially blocked

- Unknown private residences, bedrooms, hotels, isolated properties, and overnight work
- Transportation provided by the poster
- Dangerous heights, roofing, hazardous chemicals, firearms, weapons, alcohol, drugs, or high-risk machinery
- Sexual or adult services, secrecy requirements, unsupervised sensitive-location access, or unreviewable risk
- Work that conflicts with age, schedule, licensing, local youth-labor rules, or the pilot's approved category/location list

## Server decision

Job eligibility is server-owned. The job trigger defaults unapproved or risky jobs away from public eligibility, and the hosted eligibility RPC checks the current review state for application and work actions. Swift and Flutter controls explain the decision but are not the enforcement boundary.

## Poster requirements

Adults and businesses need approved pilot participation, confirmed email and phone, current training and acknowledgements, organization scope, and manual approval. Where applicable, business registration matching is shown separately from adult identity or organization approval.

## Teen protections

Exact residential information, school, partner, shelter, housing, and family details remain hidden. Safety cancellation does not automatically harm reputation. Report, block, Safety Ping, and emergency guidance are free. MORT does not guarantee payment or safety.

## Review and pause

Every allowed category needs a written task and location checklist, escalation owner, age limits, equipment rules, and incident history review. A child-safety lead can pause a job, poster, category, partner, or the pilot immediately.

# MORT Competitor Pattern Matrix

| Product/pattern | Useful evidence | MORT adaptation | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Taskrabbit screening and dispute support | Identity/background screening, evidence, support channels | Verification status, restricted evidence, case ownership, resolution history | Do not label internal checks as third-party background screening |
| Rover profile/review/messaging safety | Detailed profiles, verified reviews, on-platform messages/photos, report/block | Review provenance, participant messaging, private proof, conversation-level report/block | MORT needs stricter minor and in-person-work boundaries |
| Upwork marketplace standards | Prohibited work, fraud education, identity checks, graduated enforcement | Age-aware prohibited-job policy, scam warnings, explainable restrictions, appeal | Do not import adult remote-work assumptions or off-platform payment rules blindly |
| Nextdoor local utility | Alerts, recommendations, events, listings, businesses, public agencies | Local opportunity board, community event crews, weather guidance, partner pages | Avoid a general social feed and public teen-location exposure |
| Financial habit apps | Goals, progress, parent visibility, educational prompts | Earnings goals, preference-only records, optional guardian summaries, plain-language education | No banking claims, custody, escrow, or fake balances |
| Creator/portfolio products | Work samples, attribution, progress history, share controls | Private-by-default teen portfolio, references, resume bridge, public-safe share scope | Portfolio remains roadmap-only until schema, consent, retention, and moderation are approved |
| Productivity apps | Saved views, reminders, calendar, templates, recovery | Availability, reusable jobs, quiet hours, drafts, idempotent actions | Avoid notification overload and screen-time optimization |
| Apple platform patterns | Contextual permissions, state restoration, accessibility, notification agency | Native permissions at point of use, deep-link restoration, VoiceOver/Dynamic Type, category settings | Requires Mac and physical-iPhone verification before release claims |

The matrix identifies general patterns only. No competitor visual system, wording, asset, code, or proprietary ranking logic is copied.

# MORT Sexual Safety Runbook

> **DRAFT — NOT ATTORNEY REVIEWED OR LEGALLY APPROVED**

## Trigger

Sexual content involving a minor, grooming, sexual solicitation, coercion, assault, unwanted touching, image abuse, or credible boundary escalation.

## Actions

1. Center immediate safety and emergency services where danger is present.
2. Do not blame, interrogate, demand repeated retelling, or require the affected person to confront anyone.
3. Preserve minimally necessary evidence and restrict access to assigned trained staff.
4. Follow applicable mandatory-reporting and child-safety escalation only after qualified policy review; do not improvise legal conclusions.
5. Contain accounts and communication channels as necessary without publishing accusations.
6. Offer accessible support, appeal, and privacy-preserving follow-up.

## Boundaries

Staff preserve facts, protect affected people, and use qualified escalation. They do not confront a suspected offender, promise secrecy they cannot keep, conduct amateur criminal investigations, give individualized legal advice, publish accusations, or represent that this draft is legally approved. Every access and disclosure is minimized and logged.

# MORT Community Partnership Plan

## Eligible Partner Types

Schools, counselors, clubs, libraries, nonprofits, youth programs, local businesses, event organizers, and public/community agencies may be evaluated for bounded roles. A partner badge must identify what was actually verified and when; it is not a safety guarantee.

## Partner Workflow

1. Intake with organization identity, responsible contact, purpose, jurisdiction, and intended opportunity types.
2. Human verification and prohibited-work review.
3. Least-privilege partner role and public-safe organization page.
4. Approved job templates, event rosters, reporting, and support route.
5. Renewal, contact-change, incident, suspension, and appeal process.
6. Privacy-preserving outcome summary and periodic access review.

## Safeguards

- Partners cannot browse private teen data, messages, proof, guardian links, or exact location.
- Partner status cannot bypass job policy, age rules, reports, blocks, or moderation.
- No school or guardian can silently enroll a teen or create an account on the teen's behalf without reviewed consent flows.
- Community-service hours require source and correction; MORT does not certify legal or academic acceptance.
- Real launch requires named partner support owners and incident escalation contacts.

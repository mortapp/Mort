# Teen Safety Review Checklist

- Confirm 13-17 DOB enforcement and under-13 denial.
- Confirm exact addresses stay out of profiles/feed/chat until a safe authorized stage.
- Confirm prohibited jobs, off-platform pressure, contact sharing, scams, sexual content, and threats are moderated.
- Confirm report, block, Safety Ping, safe messaging, and basic Guardian Mode remain free.
- Confirm guardians see linked-teen safety context without unrestricted surveillance.
- Confirm adult/business verification labels never promise absolute safety.
- Confirm admin actions are backend-authorized and audited.
- Confirm Safety Ping copy never claims emergency dispatch.
- Conduct external teen-safety, legal, privacy, and abuse-response review before real users.

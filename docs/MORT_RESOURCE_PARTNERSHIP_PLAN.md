# MORT Resource Partnership Plan

Status: directory governance draft. Listings do not constitute endorsement, availability, or legal approval.

## Categories

The private directory may include school homeless-education liaisons, youth shelters, food and transportation assistance, work clothing/equipment, healthcare, mental-health support, legal aid, education support, job training, apprenticeships, YouthBuild-type programs, workforce programs, community organizations, document replacement assistance, housing support, and emergency resources.

## Source standard

Each entry needs an official source URL or documented review, organization name, category, general service area, concise summary, emergency limitation, review date, reviewer, and source status. `availability_claimed` remains false because MORT cannot promise real-time capacity.

## Privacy

Browsing and bookmarks are not public. Bookmarks are owner-only under RLS and must not feed job ranking, ads, partner rosters, public activity, vulnerability inference, or Support Circle sharing without explicit permission. Location-aware results require consent and should use the least precise area needed.

## Maintenance

Provide private outdated-resource reporting, review changed domains and contact details, expire stale entries, and remove unsafe or misleading listings. Never copy a user's resource use into an organization lead list.

## Emergency boundary

Every relevant entry states that MORT does not replace emergency services and does not guarantee availability. A directory is not a crisis-dispatch or case-management system.

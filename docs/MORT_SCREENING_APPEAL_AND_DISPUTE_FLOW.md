# MORT Screening Appeal And Dispute Flow

Status: draft. No screening vendor or screening decision is active.

## Separate Paths

- Report accuracy dispute: challenges incomplete, mixed, outdated, unverifiable, or inaccurate information with the consumer reporting agency.
- MORT policy appeal: accepts the report data for discussion but challenges MORT's interpretation, consistency, relevance, or process.
- Identity mismatch: asserts that the report concerns another person or an account was impersonated; this also creates a restricted safety/security case.

## Intake

The user can select one or more paths, explain the issue, identify disputed items without re-uploading a full report when avoidable, request accessibility or language support, and obtain a case reference. Intake must not require Guardian Mode.

## Processing

1. Mark the screening state `dispute_pending` and pause a non-final decision where required.
2. Restrict access to screening/legal reviewers and log every report access.
3. Send provider disputes through the provider's approved process and record reference and deadlines.
4. Let the user add context, disposition records, rehabilitation information, or identity evidence through private, purpose-specific upload routes.
5. Prohibit retaliation, repeated fees, or reduced reputation merely for disputing.
6. Require a different reviewer for the policy appeal when staffing permits.

## Resolution

Provide a written outcome: corrected/reopened, policy decision changed, policy decision upheld, more information required, or provider investigation pending. Explain the next step without publishing sensitive history. A corrected provider report must replace, not silently coexist with, a stale decision input.

## Records

Retain the minimum dispute request, provider reference, notices, delivery evidence, policy version, reviewer, decision, and dates under the approved schedule. Raw reports remain restricted and must not enter ordinary app analytics or mobile logs.

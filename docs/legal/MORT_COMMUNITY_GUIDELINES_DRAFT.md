## Purpose

MORT is intended to help people ages 13 through 17 find age-appropriate local work offered by adults and businesses. These guidelines describe expected conduct in jobs, applications, profiles, messages, reviews, support, and safety reports. They do not replace emergency services, employment law, youth-labor rules, or the separate MORT Safety Rules.

## Respectful participation

Users must communicate honestly and respectfully. Harassment, bullying, threats, stalking, hate or discriminatory abuse, sexual content, grooming, coercion, retaliation, impersonation, extortion, and attempts to obtain a minor's private contact information are prohibited. Adults must maintain age-appropriate boundaries and must not pressure a teen to move a conversation off MORT, keep secrets from trusted adults, meet outside the agreed job context, or perform undisclosed work.

Profiles, job posts, applications, reviews, and messages must be truthful and relevant. Users may not post scams, misleading pay claims, fake credentials, spam, unsafe instructions, unlawful goods or services, or another person's private information. Reviews must describe a genuine MORT interaction and may not be used to threaten, shame, or retaliate against someone who reported a concern or exercised a safety right.

## Job conduct

Posters must clearly describe the work, location type, schedule, expected people present, equipment, known hazards, and payment preference before acceptance. A teen may refuse undisclosed work, leave when they feel unsafe, contact a trusted person, or seek emergency help. No user may punish or pressure someone for using report, block, cancel, Safety Ping, check-in, evidence, dispute, or appeal tools.

The MORT Prohibited Work Policy controls job-category restrictions. A job that is unlawful, sexually exploitative, isolated or otherwise unsafe for a minor, materially different from the accepted scope, or prohibited by applicable youth-labor rules must not be posted or performed.

## Privacy and platform integrity

Users must not solicit passwords, job PINs, government identifiers, financial credentials, exact home or school addresses in ordinary chat, intimate images, or unnecessary location data. Attempts to bypass account restrictions, moderation, age gates, identity checks, rate limits, evidence controls, or closed-marketplace controls are prohibited.

## Reports, enforcement, and appeals

Users may report content, jobs, messages, accounts, or conduct and may block another user without paying. MORT may preserve relevant records, limit features, remove content, suspend or ban an account, or refer an urgent matter to an assigned trained reviewer. Enforcement must use an allowed action, reason code, audit record, and an appeal path where applicable. An allegation or automated signal is not a finding of guilt, and AI must not independently decide enforcement.

Emergency danger should be reported to local emergency services. MORT does not promise 24/7 human monitoring. Public marketplace access remains closed until legal, safety, verification, and staffing gates are approved.

## Review required before publication

Licensed counsel and qualified youth-safety reviewers must confirm age eligibility, enforceability for minors, UGC moderation duties, prohibited conduct, reporting and appeal language, retaliation protections, jurisdiction-specific obligations, and consistency with the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Safety Rules, and Play disclosures. Any approved publication requires a new immutable version, exact content hash, effective date, counsel reference, owner approval, and re-consent rules.

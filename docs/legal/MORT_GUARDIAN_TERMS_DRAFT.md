## Optional Guardian Mode

Guardian Mode is an optional account-linking feature intended to help a teen share selected MORT activity with a trusted adult. It is not a representation that the linked adult has legal custody, parental authority, or authority to consent on behalf of the teen. Any legally required parental consent must be configured separately by jurisdiction and may not be inferred from use of Guardian Mode.

A guardian account must be held by an adult and linked through the server-authorized invitation and acceptance flow. A guardian may not create a link solely from knowledge of a teen's name, email address, or profile. The teen and guardian must be able to see link status and use the approved unlink process, subject to any preservation or legal requirements.

## Information and controls

A linked guardian may receive only the data and controls expressly authorized by current MORT policy and the link settings. This may include approximate job status, scheduled check-ins, selected emergency contacts, safety notifications, and selected preferences. Guardian Mode does not automatically disclose exact job locations, private chat content, raw evidence, identity documents, payment credentials, passwords, or job PINs.

MORT must explain each permission in plain language and record permission changes. Location sharing, if enabled, must be time-limited, purpose-limited, visible, revocable, and available only during an authorized context. Guardians must use information for the teen's safety and must not publish, sell, exploit, or use it to harass or retaliate against any participant.

## Guardian responsibilities and limits

Guardians should keep their account and device secure, review notifications promptly, maintain accurate emergency contact information, and call local emergency services when danger may be immediate. They must not impersonate the teen, submit false reports, send messages as the teen, disclose job PINs, bypass the teen's account security, or use the platform to arrange prohibited work.

MORT does not promise that Guardian Mode will prevent harm, replace direct communication, establish legal supervision, or provide 24/7 monitoring. Basic Guardian Mode, report, block, Safety Ping, and emergency information must not require payment. Optional paid guardian conveniences, if ever offered, may not restrict core safety functions.

## Privacy, retention, and deletion

Guardian link records, permission changes, safety notifications, and access events are processed under the applicable Privacy Policy and retention schedule. Account deletion and unlinking must explain which records are deleted, de-identified, or retained for security, disputes, legal obligations, or evidence preservation. A guardian may not demand disclosure of data they are not legally and technically authorized to receive.

## Reports and appeals

A guardian may submit a report through authorized controls. Reports and automated signals are allegations or routing inputs, not findings of guilt. Enforcement uses scoped human authority, reason codes, audit logs, and appeals where applicable. MORT may restrict or revoke Guardian Mode when the link is misused or creates a safety concern.

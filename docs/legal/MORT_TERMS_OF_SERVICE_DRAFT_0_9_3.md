# MORT Terms of Service Draft 0.9.3

## Scope

MORT is a closed-pilot marketplace for local work opportunities involving teens ages 13-17 and adult posters. Public marketplace access and live payments remain disabled. These draft terms do not replace jurisdiction-specific review of child labor, classification, wage, tax, privacy, or payments obligations.

## Funded jobs and completion

For a Stripe-enabled job, the adult funds the exact accepted agreement before work begins. The displayed total separates teen earnings and the MORT service fee. Funds remain a funded payment pending release; MORT does not describe or provide escrow. MORT does not promise or guarantee work quality.

Start and finish PINs record participant actions and server timestamps. They are not identity guarantees, quality findings, legal signatures, or proof that every term was satisfied. A finish confirmation begins a review window. Payment release may be delayed during a dispute.

Adult cancellation after work begins may result in partial teen compensation under an approved policy and human review. A teen who abandons a non-safety job may receive no payment or partial payment after review. A safety-related cancellation must not automatically cause a penalty.

## Evidence, decisions, and providers

Evidence submission does not guarantee an outcome. MORT may review relevant records and authorized in-app evidence under the accepted policy. MORT platform decisions are not court judgments or criminal findings, and an appeal may be available.

Stripe and connected-account eligibility still apply. A payment provider may require guardian participation for a minor. MORT Support and automated support are not emergency, medical, or legal services. Email is a manual fallback; users must not email passwords, codes, card data, exact residential addresses, or identity documents.

Ordinary support data and active-dispute evidence have different retention and preservation periods. Terms change only through versioning and required reacceptance. This draft is inactive and creates no acceptance requirement.

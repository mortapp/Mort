# MORT Healthy Engagement Policy

MORT optimizes for useful local-work outcomes and user agency, not compulsive attention.

## Required Behavior

- Feeds are finite or intentionally paginated and can explain ranking.
- Notifications are category-based, opt-in where required, quiet-hour aware, deduplicated, and directly actionable.
- Marketing notifications require separate consent and never use time-sensitive delivery.
- Goals, progress, and celebrations reflect real user-owned outcomes.
- Users can pause alerts, saved searches, goals, Guardian summaries, and promotional communication without punishment.
- School, health, family, accessibility, and rest boundaries take priority over engagement targets.
- Paywalls are dismissible, show honest SDK-returned products/prices, preserve the free path, and never manufacture scarcity.

## Prohibited Behavior

- Punitive streak loss or shame
- Infinite autoplay or unread inflation
- Fake countdowns, fake discounts, or repeated interruption
- Default public sharing or location disclosure
- Safety anxiety used to sell a subscription
- Rankings that humiliate low earners or inexperienced teens
- Guardian pressure as a growth loop
- AI-generated urgency presented as fact

Product experiments must define a harm hypothesis, stop condition, privacy scope, and free-path measure before exposure.

# MORT Teen Participant Terms Draft 0.9.3

## Teen participation

MORT is for teens ages 13-17 in the closed pilot. A teen may stop and use the Safety Center when a person, place, task, or situation does not match the agreement or feels unsafe. Safety reporting and blocking remain available regardless of payment or support status.

For a Stripe-enabled job, the adult funds the accepted agreement before work begins. MORT does not guarantee job quality, payment, transfer eligibility, or a review outcome. A funded payment remains pending release and is not escrow. Disputes may delay release.

Start and finish PINs should be exchanged in person and never posted publicly. They record actions, not identity or job-quality guarantees. If a finish PIN is refused, a teen may submit a factual statement and limited evidence; that submission does not guarantee payment.

Adult cancellation after work starts may produce partial compensation after review. Final, confirmed non-safety abandonment may produce no or partial payment and a short closed-test cooldown. Safety cancellation must not cause an automatic penalty.

## Evidence and help

MORT may review relevant authorized MORT records and evidence under policy. Decisions are not court judgments or criminal findings, and appeals may be available. Stripe eligibility and provider-required guardian steps may apply independently from optional Guardian Mode.

Support and automated help are not emergency, medical, or legal services. Email is a manual fallback; never email passwords, codes, full card numbers, addresses, or identity documents. Dispute evidence can be preserved longer than ordinary support messages. Terms change by version and required reacceptance. This draft is inactive.
